WHAT CHANGED
- Fill screen is now the default. It preserves the image's proportions,
  enlarging it enough to cover the screen and cropping the excess edges.
  It does not stretch the image or add an artificial blur effect.
- Move the pointer or tap to reveal controls. The top-left Image sizing menu
  offers Fill screen and Fit whole image. Fit shows every edge, with margins
  when the image and screen proportions differ.
- The choice stays as you browse photos during this page session; a reload
  starts with Fill screen again. Switching modes recenters and resets zoom.
- Zoom/pan remain available. With the image focused, 0 resets the selected
  sizing mode. Double-click/tap toggles zoom/reset. Zooming pauses slideshow.
- The quiet bottom-center stadium info box sits a little lower, keeping an
  eight-pixel gap above the device's safe-area bottom.

Resolution caveat: enlarging an image to fill a large/high-resolution screen
can reveal softness or existing detail limits. Fill cannot create extra detail.
Choose Fit if you prefer the entire image with less enlargement.

All 258 schools / 519 views, state filters, division labels, records behavior,
slideshow scope and grouped shuffle are preserved. No new artwork is included.
